The evidence

A multicentre RCT found that combining OR ergonomics with supervised exercise significantly reduced low back pain and halved analgesic use at six months. Giagio et al., 2019
MSK pain prevalence ranges from 51.7 to 97 percent, with lower back, neck, and shoulder pain dominating. Richard et al., 2025
