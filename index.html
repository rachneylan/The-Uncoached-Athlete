06 The Paradigm Shift
The Paradigm Shift

From endurance to readiness.

From

  • Performance as a personal trait
  • Outcomes attributed to volume and experience
  • Human performance as background
  • Fatigue managed individually
  • Coaching and conditioning as extracurricular

To

  • Performance as a system
  • Outcomes shaped by measurable inputs
  • Human performance as a clinical variable
  • Fatigue as a managed parameter
  • Coaching and conditioning as infrastructure
Outcomes are shaped by the system around the performer. The next eight slides walk through what that system looks like.
The Uncoached Athlete 06 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026
01 of 08 · Sleep
No other recovery input returns as much as sleep.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
01
The Uncoached Athlete 07 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

Sleep relative to individual baseline produced the largest improvements in next-day HRV — the highest-leverage recovery input available. Jevsevar et al., 2021
Disrupted or insufficient sleep degrades performance in simulation and in real procedures alike. Shin et al., 2024

In your week

Anchor your wake time — consistency is the lever
20-minute nap pre-list where schedule allows
Caffeine cut-off 8 hours before sleep
The athletes who win are not the ones who train through the night. They are the ones who protect it.
02 of 08 · Neurobiology and Regulation
Arousal is regulated, not endured. The optimum is measurable.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
02
The Uncoached Athlete 08 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

HRV is a validated real-time biomarker of stress and resilience in surgeons. Thé et al., 2020; Nandhra et al., 2024
Higher sympathovagal balance in the first five minutes of surgery was associated with fewer major complications. Awtry et al., 2025
Optimal performance is not the absence of stress. It is its regulation.

In your week

4-7-8 breath pre or between cases (4 inhale · 7 hold · 8 exhale)
Pre-scrub visualisation, 60 seconds
HRV-guided readiness check on demanding list days
Calm is not the goal. Control is.
03 of 08 · Nutrition
Stable substrate, stable performance.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
03
The Uncoached Athlete 09 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

Nutritional habits directly affect surgical performance, longevity, and decision-making under fatigue. Faran et al., 2025
Irregular meal patterns, skipped meals, and stress eating are common — institutional support is essential. Como et al., 2025

In your week

Pre-list protein and fibre anchored meal
Prepare or pre-order lunch — remove the decision
One-handed snacks between cases: glucose and fructose (dried fruit)
Avoid alcohol before long list days
A long list is an endurance event. Almost no one fuels it like one.
04 of 08 · Hydration
Concentration, reaction time, and judgement all sit downstream of hydration.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
04
The Uncoached Athlete 10 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

Dehydration impairs concentration, reaction time, and decision-making. Faran et al., 2025
The barriers have historically been operational and workload-driven — not a lack of awareness. Qi et al., 2026

In your week

Pre-list hydration target — not post-list catch-up
Electrolyte-anchored fluids on long lists
Build into workflow — reduce reliance on memory
Cognitive fatigue at 3pm is often just dehydration wearing a disguise.
05 of 08 · MSK + Ergonomics
The body that delivers the surgery is an asset. It needs to be protected.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
05
The Uncoached Athlete 11 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

A multicentre RCT found that combining OR ergonomics with supervised exercise significantly reduced low back pain and halved analgesic use at six months. Giagio et al., 2019
MSK pain prevalence ranges from 51.7 to 97 percent, with lower back, neck, and shoulder pain dominating. Richard et al., 2025

In your week

Table height calibrated to elbow, not assumption
90 seconds of micro-movement between cases
Helmet and headlamp ergonomic audit
Impact mat on every list
The career usually ends because of the body, not the mind. That timeline is now modifiable.
06 of 08 · Physical Conditioning
Conditioning is a determinant of resilience, recovery, and longevity.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
06
The Uncoached Athlete 12 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

Daily physical activity improves HRV, reduces stress, and is linked to better cardiovascular and MSK outcomes across surgical careers. Qi et al., 2026
Structured strength training reduces injury risk in surgeons. Dairywala et al., 2021

In your week

Two strength sessions weekly — posterior chain priority
Aerobic base 150 minutes weekly, broken to fit the roster
Mobility for thoracic spine, hips, and shoulders
You are already an athlete. This is about training like one on purpose.
07 of 08 · Recovery
Recovery is where capacity is rebuilt. It is part of the work, not a pause from it.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
07
The Uncoached Athlete 13 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

Recovery infrastructure is consistently identified as the dominant gap in surgeon well-being literature, with short-term programs producing short-term results. Vitous et al., 2023
Evidence-based recovery protocols spanning physical and cognitive domains are ready to implement. Hasan et al., 2026

In your week

Post-list decompression: 10 minutes, breath, light movement, fluid, fuel
One full recovery day protected weekly — non-negotiable
Annual leave used, not banked
In sport, recovery is training. The same logic holds in surgery.
08 of 08 · Leadership + Team
Non-technical performance shapes outcomes as much as technical skill — and it is trainable.
The Stack · Eight Determinants of Human Performance
08
The Uncoached Athlete 14 / 21 · © Rachel Neylan 2026

The evidence

Surgeon stress significantly degrades non-technical skills in the OR — situational awareness, communication, and leadership. Anton et al., 2021
Structured coaching and mental skills programs improve resilience, teamwork, and intraoperative decision-making. Stefanidis et al., 2017; Wetzel et al., 2011

In your week

60-second pre-list team brief
Post-list debrief: technical and non-technical
One named environmental variable owned by the team
The best team in the building is the one that runs well when you step away from the table.
15 The Opportunity
Where the field is heading

The science is here. A profession in transition.

01
Stress and Arousal Regulation

Now treated as a measurable, modifiable performance variable. The translation into surgical practice is underway.

HRV validated as real-time biomarker of surgical performance · Awtry et al., 2025
02
Sleep and Fatigue

Established as the highest-leverage recovery determinant. The science is mature. The infrastructure is the next step.

Largest improvements in next-day HRV and procedure performance · Jevsevar et al., 2021
03
Musculoskeletal Health

Prevalence data and intervention evidence are now substantial. Career longevity is increasingly seen as a system outcome.

51–97% prevalence across surgical specialties · Richard et al., 2025
04
Recovery

Operationalised in every elite domain. The translation into surgical workflow is the current frontier.

Dominant gap in surgeon well-being literature · Vitous et al., 2023
05
OR Environment and Cognitive Load

Measurable in real time, with validated tools. The data is producing a new generation of design questions.

Stress degrades NTS including situational awareness · Anton et al., 2021
06
Career Longevity

Now framed as a workforce sustainability issue, not a personal endurance issue.

Physical activity the only modifiable risk factor for MSK disorders · Giagio et al., 2019
The science is concrete. The translation into surgical practice is now the work.
